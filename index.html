My Story

Every song on this label started as something I wrote alone, long before there was a label, a website, or a roster to put it on. I believe the instrument for it — the words, the melodies, the drive to keep writing even when nothing else was working — was given to me by YHVH through Yeshua. Micdom AI Records exists because that gift needed somewhere to go.

I spent twenty years as a celibate priest, and years of trial and isolation that tested me more than I can fully put into words here. What I can tell you is that I came out the other side of it with a clearer sense of calling than I went in with — the Sphinx, the Holy Spirit, call it what fits your own framework — I know what carried me, and I know it wasn't me alone.

None of this started with me, either. I carry the name and the example of my grandparents — Mama Hajah Norfeh and Colonel Ahamadu Varfee Sirleaf — and everything built here is meant to be a legacy that outlasts me, the way theirs outlasted them.
